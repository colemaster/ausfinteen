QLD Graduated Licensing Path (Under 25)

Learner (L) (L plates)

Age 16+ • 12 months minimum

Fees: PrepL $29.70 + learner licence $80.15 (3 yrs)

Requirements: Pass PrepL online road rules test at 16. Under 25s complete 100 logbook hours (10 at night) via logbook app, submit 12 business days before test, 3-for-1 instructor bonus max 10 hrs.

Restrictions: Must be supervised by an open-licence driver (1yr open licence), display L plates, 0.00 BAC.

Provisional P1 (Red P) (Red P plates)

Age 17+ • 12 months

Fees: HPT $42.70 + practical test $69.40 + P1 licence $94.65 (1 yr)

Requirements: Hold Ls 12 months, finish 100 logbook hours, pass hazard perception test + practical driving test (Q-SAFE).

Restrictions: Under 25: total phone ban, max 1 peer passenger 11pm–5am, no high-powered vehicles, 0.00 BAC.

Provisional P2 (Green P) (Green P plates)

Age 18+ • 12–24 months (age dependent)

Fees: P2 licence $132.00 (2 yrs) — $94.65 (1 yr) option also available

Requirements: Hold P1 for 12 months with a clean record. No extra test needed.

Restrictions: Hands-free phone OK, no passenger limits, no high-powered vehicles (under 25), 0.00 BAC.

Open Licence (No plates)

Age 20+ • Unrestricted

Fees: Open licence $94.65 (1 yr) / $132.00 (2 yrs) / $163.25 (3 yrs) / $188.95 (4 yrs) / $212.00 (5 yrs)

Requirements: Hold P2 for the required period (min. age 20 for under-25 starters).

Restrictions: Normal road rules apply. No special conditions. GIR 3.4% indexation 2026-27.

Cash Cars Beat Dealer Loans

A $4,500 cash car avoids 3 years of 12.5% dealer interest and forced full insurance. Always do a $2 PPSR check first.

EV Only Saves If You Charge At Home

Home off-peak EV charging is ~2.5× cheaper than petrol; public fast chargers cost about the same as petrol.

What Will I Learn in Cars & Driving?

Queensland uses a Graduated Licensing Scheme (GLS) designed to build safe driving habits over time: 1) Age 15 & 11 months: Enrol in the online PrepL road rules course. 2) Age 16: Pass PrepL and obtain your Learner (L) licence ($82.90 for 3 years). Hold for at least 12 months and complete 100 logbook hours (including 10 at night). 3) Age 17: Pass the online Hazard Perception Test ($44.15) and the practical driving test ($71.75) to earn your Provisional P1 (Red P) licence ($97.85/1yr). 4) Age 18: Move to Provisional P2 (Green P) licence ($136.50/2yrs) after holding P1 for 12 months with a clean record. 5) Age 20: Upgrade to a full Open Licence ($97.85/1yr or $218.00/5yrs).
