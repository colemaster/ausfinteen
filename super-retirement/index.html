What Will I Learn in Super & Retirement?

Superannuation is compulsory long-term investment money your employer pays on top of your wage into a tax-sheltered fund for your future. Effective 1 July 2025, the statutory Super Guarantee (SG) rate is legislated at 12.0% for FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27. Super is not deducted from your base take-home wage—it is an additional 12.0% on top of your Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE). For example, if you earn $500 gross in a qualifying week, your employer must deposit an extra $60 directly into your super fund.

1% ACTION STEPCheck your payslip every payday to verify your employer is calculating 12.0% super on your gross earnings.
