Emergency Runway & Macroeconomic Stress Resilience Guide

Because mortgage offset interest savings are 100% tax-free, you must earn a substantially higher pre-tax return in a High Interest Savings Account (HISA) to match it. The exact formula is Pre-Tax Equivalent Return = Mortgage Rate / (1 - Marginal Tax Rate). For example, at a 32% marginal rate and a 6.2% mortgage, a HISA must pay 9.12% gross return to break even!

1% ACTION STEPKeep your primary liquid savings buffer in your mortgage offset account.
