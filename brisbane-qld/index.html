QLD State Rules That Matter for Teens

🔑

Rental bond = max 4 weeks

In Queensland your bond is strictly capped at 4 weeks rent for all residential tenancies (the former $700/week threshold was abolished). The lessor/agent must lodge it with the RTA within 10 days using Form 2 or RTA Web Services.

📅

Rent increases capped to 12 months

Rent can only be increased once every 12 months in Queensland, and the cap is attached to the PROPERTY itself, not the tenant. If a previous tenant vacated after an increase, rent cannot be hiked until 12 full months have elapsed. 2 months written notice is mandatory.

🚫

Rent bidding banned by law

Landlords and real estate agents cannot advertise properties without a fixed price, solicit rental bids above the listed price, or accept offers higher than advertised under the RTRA Act.

🚌

50c flat fares permanent

Queensland 50-cent flat fares are permanent by statutory enactment across all TransLink public transport (buses, QR Citytrain lines, Brisbane CityCats & ferries, Gold Coast G:link tram, and 9 regional city bus networks including Cairns, Townsville, Toowoomba, Mackay, Rockhampton, Bundaberg, Gladstone, and Hervey Bay/Maryborough). Free weekend travel for primary school students on Child Go Card. Airtrain excluded. Tap on/off with contactless Visa/Mastercard or Go Card. Free transfers within 60 minutes.

💳

Smart Ticketing: Visa & Mastercard

Tap on/off with contactless physical Visa/Mastercard or digital phone wallets (Apple/Google/Samsung Pay). American Express was discontinued on 15 December 2025 across all readers. Always tap on and off with the exact same device or card to avoid card clash and double default fares.

🚗

QLD rego = 3 parts (CTP by car class)

Queensland rego combines the registration fee, traffic improvement fee, and CTP insurance. CTP is priced on vehicle class (not driver age) — saving young drivers hundreds compared to NSW Greenslips.

🪪

Learner licence from 16 (PrepL)

You can get your Ls at 16 in Queensland via the PrepL online road rules test ($29.70). Under 25s must log 100 supervised driving hours (incl. 10 at night; 10 professional lessons count as 30 hours) before the P1 practical driving test.

⭐️

Super for under 18s

Same ATO rule nationwide: under 18, employers must pay 12% super only if you work more than 30 hours in a calendar week (SGAA 1992 s 19(10)). Over 18, super is paid on all eligible hours.

🎉

Public holiday penalty rates

QLD public holidays (Easter, Ekka Show Day in August, Labour Day, Christmas) earn casual penalty rates of +125% to +150% (225%–250% total pay) — a substantial weekend earnings boost for young workers.

What Will I Learn in Brisbane, QLD?

(17 topics)

A student sharehouse room within 5km of the CBD costs about $286/week (2026-27 median), with middle-ring suburbs (Moorooka, Salisbury, Chermside, Nathan) ranging from $200–$260/week and inner riverfront pockets (West End, St Lucia, New Farm) reaching $350–$500/week. Weekly groceries average $95–$115 when shopping at Aldi, Woolworths specials, and the Rocklea Sunday or West End markets. Shared utilities (electricity, water, gas) and NBN 50 are roughly $35–$45/week. Public transport is a flat 50¢ per trip across all Translink SEQ buses, trains, ferries, and trams (~$5.00/week for 10 trips). A budget mobile SIM (Boost/Aldi Mobile) is $15–$25/month ($4–$6/week), while dining out and entertainment typically run $50–$80/week. A realistic total student budget is $440–$540/week in middle-ring corridors or $580–$720/week in inner-city riverfront hubs — substantially more affordable than Sydney ($700–$900/week) or Melbourne ($620–$780/week).
