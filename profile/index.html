First Job, TFN & Medicare Independence

15yo First Job: Fast Food / Retail Junior (Casual)

Junior Award Rate
$13.90/hr

At 15, you can legally work in retail and fast food nationwide without special employer permits (e.g. Victorian child employment permits end at 15). You also unlock healthcare independence: Services Australia allows you to order your own separate green Medicare card (via Form MS004, MS011, or myGov) for 100% confidential doctor visits without parental notification. It is time to lock down your first paycheck budget.

Employment & Hours

You must receive at least the Fast Food / General Retail Level 1 junior award rate ($13.90/hr casual). Entitled to a paid 10-minute rest pause for shifts over 4 hours, and 3-hour minimum pay per shift (or 1.5h on school days in Retail with consent).

Superannuation Rule

Under Section 11 of the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992, workers under 18 only receive 12.0% employer superannuation if they work more than 30 hours in a given week. Casuals working 8 hrs/wk do not receive compulsory super unless an EBA mandates it.

Tax & Threshold

Claim the Tax-Free Threshold on Question 8 of your ATO TFN Declaration (NAT 3092). Because you earn $5,782/year (8 hrs @ $13.90), your annual income is well below the $22,575 effective tax-free threshold. Estimated PAYG withholding is $0.

Watch Out: Age 15 Warning Flags & Traps

Crucial Australian legal, tax, and debt traps specific to your age bracket.

Under-18 Super Guarantee Exemption Confusion
info

Australian law does not mandate 12% super for workers under 18 working 30 hours or less per week.

Fix: Do not panic if super is $0 on payslips unless you work >30 hours in a week or your specific enterprise agreement pays it.
Flat $10/hr Cash-in-Hand Rate
high

Suburban cafes frequently underpay 15-year-olds with flat cash rates, dodging weekend penalty rates ($17.38-$20.85/hr).

Fix: Know your modern award rights. You are legally entitled to $13.90/hr weekdays and penalty rates on weekends/public holidays.
The 3-Hour Minimum Shift Rule Violation
medium

Managers cannot roster or pay you for less than 3 hours under Fast Food & Retail Awards, even if sent home early.

Fix: If sent home after 1.5 hours because it is quiet, politely remind payroll that award conditions require 3 hours minimum pay.
Unpaid Off-The-Clock Cleaning
medium

Being forced to clock out at 9:00pm but stay until 9:45pm to mop floors or complete closing tasks without pay.

Fix: All mandatory work activities must be recorded on the timeclock and fully compensated down to the minute.

Step 1 • Legal Prerequisite
ATO NAT 3092 / AusPost

Apply for Your Free Tax File Number (TFN)

Apply online through Australia Post or myGov using your 100-point ID (Birth Certificate [70pts] + School Student ID [40pts] = 110pts). It is 100% free (never pay a third-party fee!). Giving your TFN to your employer (via ATO Form NAT 3092) and bank stops 47% top marginal withholding tax.

Checkpoints: 0 of 5 done
Action Checkpoints:
Gather 100 points of ID (Australian Birth Certificate [70 pts] + School Photo ID [40 pts] = 110 pts)
Complete the online TFN application for Australian residents on ato.gov.au
Print or save the official application summary barcode
Book and attend your free 5-minute identity appointment at Australia Post
Store your 9-digit TFN securely once it arrives by mail within 28 days
Pro-Tip:

Never pay private third-party websites claiming to process a TFN for $50-$100. Applying through Australia Post and the ATO is completely free by law.
