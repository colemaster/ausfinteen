Careers & Employment

Everything you need for your first Australian job: Junior Award pay rates, Saturday/Sunday penalty rates, ATO TFN & Super forms, unpaid trial shift rules, Barefoot Investor paycheck automation, and workplace conversation scripts!

💡 2026 First Job Tips: Find jobs on Seek.com.au, Indeed, Jora, local community boards, or by dropping resumes in-store. School-based apprenticeships and traineeships are available from Year 10 (age 15). For the gig economy, note that Airtasker requires 18+, but tutoring platforms may accept 16+.

Common Student Award Roles

Preset pay = your effective junior rate (2026-27 Fast Food/Retail Level 1, $27.81/hr adult base). From 1 December 2026, 18-20yo workers move to the full adult rate after 6 months with the same employer (FWC [2026] FWCFB 75).

Award Rules: 3-hour minimum shift for casuals. 25% casual loading. Saturday: 125% perm / 150% casual; Sunday Level 1: 150% perm / 150% casual (Level 2: 160% casual, Level 3: 175% casual); Public Holiday: 225% perm / 250% casual. Late evening (Mon–Fri 10pm–midnight): 110% perm (+10% loading). Night (Mon–Fri midnight–6am): 115% perm (+15% loading). Transport reimbursement applies if shift finishes after 10pm or starts before 7am when regular transport is unavailable.
