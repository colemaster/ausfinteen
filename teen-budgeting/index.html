What Will I Learn in Budgeting & Paychecks?

For a typical 15yo casual worker earning $80–$150/week: 1) 60% ($48–$90) Daily Blow: Translink 50c fares, school canteen, phone credit, and social outings. 2) 20% ($16–$30) Mojo Safety Buffer: High-interest savings account locked away until reaching $500. 3) 20% ($16–$30) Grow / Big Goal: First car deposit, driving lessons (PrepL), or long-term investing. Automating this split on payday ensures savings happen without willpower!

1% ACTION STEPSet up 3 separate sub-accounts in your banking app and automate percentage splits each payday.
