Australian Capital Gains Tax & ITAA 1997 Rules Guide

Under Section 118-145 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997, if you move out of your main residence and rent it out to produce income, you can continue to treat it as your main residence for CGT purposes for up to 6 years of renting. If you sell within 6 years and elect the exemption (and nominate no other home as your main residence), the entire capital gain is 100% tax-free!
