Australian Government $250,000 Deposit Guarantee (APRA FCS)
100% Protected

Under the Banking Act 1959, your deposits in any APRA-regulated Australian bank or credit union are legally guaranteed by the Federal Government up to $250,000 AUD per depositor.

⚠️ Important: Always provide your Tax File Number (TFN) to the bank! Under Section 202D, banks are required to withhold 47% tax on savings interest if no TFN is attached.

What Will I Learn in Interest & Financial Products?

A High-Interest Savings Account (HISA) is a risk-free savings account designed to grow your cash faster than standard everyday transaction accounts. Top Australian youth savings accounts (e.g. BOQ Future Saver at 5.80%, Newcastle Permanent Smart Saver at 5.75%, Great Southern Bank at 5.50%, Westpac Bump, CommBank Youthsaver) pay 5.00%–5.80% p.a. interest. Furthermore, under the Australian Government Financial Claims Scheme (FCS) regulated by APRA, your deposits up to $250,000 AUD per depositor per APRA-regulated bank (Authorised Deposit-taking Institution / ADI) are 100% legally guaranteed by the Federal Government if a bank ever fails.
