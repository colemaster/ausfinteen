Money-Saving Brisbane Tips

  • • Get a concession Go Card — 50% off all public transport.
  • • Shop Aldi or the West End / Rocklea markets for cheap fresh produce.
  • • Ride the CityCat ferry or walk — it's free with your weekly cap.
  • • Hunt for rooms in Chermside, Annerley or Indooroopilly (~$250–400/wk).
  • • Split utilities & internet fairly — always get bills in writing.

QLD State Rules That Matter for Teens

🔑

Rental bond = max 4 weeks

In Queensland your bond is capped at 4 weeks rent (no rent threshold since Sep 2024). The agent must lodge it with the RTA within 10 days.

🚌

50c flat fares

Since 5 August 2024, all Queensland public transport (bus, train, ferry, tram) is a flat 50c per trip — no Go Card, no concession needed. Free transfers within 60 minutes; no daily/weekly cap required. School students no longer need a concession card.

🚗

QLD rego = 3 parts

Queensland rego combines the registration fee, traffic improvement fee, and CTP insurance. CTP is priced on your car class (not your age) — QLD has the lowest CTP in mainland Australia.

🪪

Learner licence from 16

You can get your Ls at 16 in Queensland (PrepL online road rules test). Under 25s must log 100 supervised driving hours (incl. 10 at night) before the P1 test — use the QLD Learner Logbook app to track them.

⭐️

Super for under 18s

Same ATO rule nationwide: under 18, employers must pay 12% super only if you work more than 30 hours in a calendar week. Over 18, super is on all hours.

🎉

Public holidays

QLD public holidays (Easter, Ekka Show Day in August, Christmas) earn penalty rates of +125% to +150% for casuals — a great weekend pay boost.

What Will I Learn in Brisbane, QLD?

A student sharehouse room within 5km of the CBD costs about $286/week (2026 median), groceries $100/week, utilities & internet $40/week, and public transport is just 50¢ per trip under Translink flat fares (~$5/week). A realistic student budget is around $500–$540 per week total — substantially cheaper than Sydney or Melbourne.
