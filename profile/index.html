The 15-Year-Old Australian Independence Roadmap

Your 6 essential financial independence milestones: TFN, High-Interest Banking, Medicare, First Casual Job, Ls Prep, and Student Perks.

0%Progress
Step 1 • Legal Prerequisite

Apply for Your Free Tax File Number (TFN)

Apply online through Australia Post or myGov. It is 100% free (never pay a third-party fee!). Stop 47% tax from being withheld.

Book a free identity appointment at Australia Post with your Birth Certificate & Student ID.
Australia Post: TFN Application for Australian Residents & Students
Step 2 • Banking & Savings

Open a Zero-Fee 5.0%+ Youth Bank Account

Open a fee-free youth transaction account with Visa/Mastercard Debit and Apple Pay / Google Wallet. Provide your TFN to stop 47% tax on savings interest!

Compare BOQ Future Saver (5.80%), Newcastle Permanent (5.75%), or Great Southern Bank (5.50%).
Moneysmart: Bank Accounts & High-Interest Savings
Step 3 • Healthcare Independence

Claim Your Own Green Medicare Card

From age 15 in Australia, Services Australia allows you to get your own separate green Medicare card (via myGov or Form MS011) to visit bulk-billing doctors independently and manage your own health records.

Submit Services Australia Form MS011 or apply online via myGov under "Get a new card and number".
Services Australia: Getting Your Own Medicare Card at 15 (Form MS011)
Step 4 • First Paycheck

Land Your First Casual Job & Master Junior Pay

Understand your junior rate ($11.12–$15.64/hr + 25% casual loading), claim the $18,200 tax-free threshold on Form NAT 3092, and enforce the 3-hour minimum shift rule.

Use our Teen Resume Builder and submit applications to Woolies, Coles, Maccas, or sports refereeing.
Fair Work Ombudsman: Pay & Junior Award Rates Calculator
Step 5 • Driving Prep

Practice PrepL / DKT for Your Ls at 16

At 15 you can study and practice the road rules tests online (PrepL in QLD, DKT in NSW, Learner test in VIC) so you are ready to get your Ls on the exact day you turn 16!

Run free practice road rules quizzes on your state transport portal.
Queensland Government: PrepL Online Driving Course & Test
Step 6 • Real World Savings

Unlock Student Concessions & Scam Protection

Get 50c public transport fares (QLD) or Concession Opal (NSW), join Student Edge / UNiDAYS for tech discounts, and protect against money muling and task scams.

Download Student Edge and verify your high school status for student discounts.
Student Edge: Free Australian Student Discounts & Perks
