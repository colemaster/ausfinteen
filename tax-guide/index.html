What Will I Learn in Tax & Tax Returns?

In Australia, every resident individual gets the first $18,200 of taxable income 100% tax-free each financial year (1 July to 30 June). When you start a job, complete ATO Form NAT 3092 (Tax File Number Declaration) and tick "YES" to Question 8 ("Do you want to claim the tax-free threshold from this payer?"). This instructs your employer's payroll system not to withhold any tax on weekly wages up to ~$350/week ($700/fortnight).

1% ACTION STEPCheck Question 8 on your TFN Declaration form (NAT 3092) whenever starting a new job to ensure you selected YES.
