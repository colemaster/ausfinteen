The Mathematical Magic of Starting at Age 15 (15yo vs 25yo Comparison)

Time in the market beats timing the market. The money you invest between 15 and 25 has up to 50 years to compound, making every dollar worth up to 45 times its initial value at retirement!

Investor A (Starts at 15)

Contributes $30/week from age 15 to 25 (10 years). Total cash invested: $15,600. Stops adding cash at 25 and lets it compound at 8.0% p.a. until age 65.

Ending Balance at 65: $518k-$584k!
Investor B (Waits until 25)

Waits 10 years and contributes $30/week from age 25 to 65 (40 years continuous). Total cash invested: $62,400 (4X MORE CASH!).

Ending Balance at 65: ~$458,000
💡 Investor A finishes with over $126,000 MORE wealth while contributing $46,800 LESS cash. Time in the market is the ultimate financial superpower!
