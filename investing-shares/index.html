What Will I Learn in Investing & Shares?

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) lets you buy a tiny slice of dozens, hundreds, or even thousands of top companies (like Apple, BHP, Commonwealth Bank, Microsoft) in a single trade, spreading your risk automatically. Unlike traditional unlisted managed funds that price only once at the end of the day, ETFs trade live on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) just like ordinary shares during market hours (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM AEST). A passive index ETF simply tracks a market benchmark (such as the S&P/ASX 200 or S&P 500) at an ultra-low annual management fee.
