The EV vs Petrol Mathematics (2026-27 Benchmarks):

• Petrol running costs: A typical midsize petrol car or SUV consuming 9.5L/100km at $2.30/L costs $21.85 per 100km ($3,278/yr over 15,000 km). Even an efficient hatchback (7.5L/100km @ $2.38/L) costs $17.85 per 100km ($2,678/yr).

• EV electricity charging: A modern EV draws ~15 kWh/100km. At a standard residential flat rate of 28c/kWh (AER DMO benchmark, matching the ATO Safe Harbour 4.2c/km rate), driving costs just $4.20 per 100km ($630/yr). On dedicated off-peak EV night tariffs (8c–15c/kWh), that drops to only ~$1.20–$1.50 per 100km ($225/yr)!

• Annual fuel savings over 15,000 km: You save $2,648+ per year compared to a 9.5L petrol car on flat tariffs, or over $3,050/yr on off-peak rates — putting more than $13,000 to $15,000 back in your pocket over 5 years.

* Note: Public DC ultra-fast charging (~$0.65/kWh = $9.75/100km) erodes part of the savings on road trips, while daytime rooftop solar charging reduces fuel costs to nearly $0.

QLD Graduated Licensing Path (Under 25)

Learner (L) (L plates (yellow with black L))

Age 16+ • 12 months minimum

Fees: PrepL $29.70 + learner licence $80.15 (3 yrs)

Requirements: Enrol in PrepL online from age 15y11m ($29.70, 12 months access, 100% mastery) or pass the in-person written test ($29.70). Drivers under 25 must log 100 supervised driving hours (at least 10 night hours between sunset and sunrise) in the official Queensland Learner Logbook app or hardcopy book ($25.90). 3-for-1 instructor credit: up to 10 hours of professional lessons count as 30 logbook hours. Submit logbook 12+ business days before booking the practical test.

Restrictions: Must be supervised by an open licence holder (held for 1+ year). Display L plates on front and rear (visible from 20m; $276 fine + 2 demerit points). Strictly 0.00 BAC (supervisor must be under 0.05 BAC). Zero mobile phone use for learners under 25 (no hands-free, Bluetooth, smartwatch, or loudspeaker — camera fine $1,295 + 4 demerit points).

Provisional P1 (Red P) (Red P plates (white with red P))

Age 17+ • 12 months

Fees: HPT $42.70 + practical test $69.40 + P1 licence $94.65 (1 yr)

Requirements: Hold Ls for 12 months minimum and finish 100 logbook hours. Pass the online Hazard Perception Test (HPT, $42.70, unlocked after 6 months on Ls, valid 12 months), then pass the Q-SAFE 30-minute on-road practical driving test ($69.40).

Restrictions: Display red P plates front and rear ($276 fine + 2 demerits). Strictly 0.00 BAC. Zero mobile phone use under 25 (no hands-free, wireless audio, smartwatch, or GPS touch — camera fine $1,295 + 4 demerit points). Peer passenger restriction: between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM, maximum 1 passenger under 21 who is not immediate family (penalty: $518 fine + 3 demerit points). No high-powered vehicles (>130 kW/tonne, exemption fee $51.45). Demerit point cliff: 4 points within 1 year triggers licence suspension. No blanket night curfew.

Provisional P2 (Green P) (Green P plates (white with green P))

Age 18+ • 2 years if under 25 (or 1 year if issued at 25+)

Fees: P2 licence $132.00 (2 yrs) — $94.65 (1 yr) option also available

Requirements: Hold P1 for 12 months with a clean record (no suspensions or disqualifications). Upgrades automatically to P2 without extra driving tests.

Restrictions: Display green P plates front and rear ($276 fine + 2 demerits). Strictly 0.00 BAC. Hands-free phone use permitted (e.g. phone mounted in an approved cradle for GPS navigation, Bluetooth calls). Peer passenger 11pm–5am restriction lifted. High-powered vehicle ban continues for under-25 drivers. Demerit point threshold remains 4 points within 1 year.

Open Licence (No plates required)

Age 20+ • Unrestricted (renew every 1 to 5 years)

Fees: Open licence $94.65 (1 yr) / $132.00 (2 yrs) / $163.25 (3 yrs) / $188.95 (4 yrs) / $212.00 (5 yrs)

Requirements: Hold P2 for the required period (minimum open-licence age 20 for under-25 starters; 1 year if P2 was issued at 25+). Automatically upgrades to Open.

Restrictions: Standard QLD road rules apply. Legal BAC limit under 0.05 (0.00 for heavy vehicles, buses, taxis, rideshare, tow trucks). Demerit point cap increases to 12 points in a 3-year rolling period. High-powered vehicle restrictions lifted.

QLD Road Rules, Camera Fines & Demerit Points (2026-2027 Schedule)

P-Plater Cap: 4 Points / Year
⚠️ The 4-Demerit Point Cliff for Young Drivers:

Queensland Learner and Provisional (P1 & P2) drivers have a strict threshold of 4 demerit points in a 1-year period. A single mobile phone camera fine ($1,295 + 4 demerit points), seatbelt camera offence ($1,295 + 4 points), or moderate speeding offence (21–30 km/h over: $777 + 4 points) instantly triggers an immediate 3-month licence suspension or a 1-year good driving behaviour bond!

⚡ Year-Round Double Demerits: Queensland applies double demerit points all year round for repeat offences committed within 12 months (e.g. 2nd mobile phone fine = 8 demerit points + $1,295).

Zero Alcohol Limit (0.00% BAC): Learner, P1, and P2 provisional drivers must strictly maintain 0.00% BAC (supervisors must remain under 0.05% BAC). Any detectable alcohol triggers an immediate 24-hour police suspension, mandatory Magistrates Court appearance, minimum 3 to 9 months licence disqualification, and up to 14 penalty units ($2,417) in court fines.

Peer Passenger Restriction (P1 under 25): Between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM, you are restricted to a maximum of 1 peer passenger under 21 who is not an immediate family member. Penalty: $518 fine (3 penalty units @ $172.70) + 3 demerit points, leaving only a 1-point margin before licence suspension.

Plate Display Rules: L and P plates must be clean, undamaged, and visible from 20 metres front and rear. Failing to display plates (or displaying plates when an open licence holder is driving) carries a $276 fine + 2 demerit points.

P's By 17, Full Licence By 20

Start Ls at 16, knock out 100 logbook hours, pass HPT + practical, then P1 → P2 → Open. Total gov fees ≈ $543.

Cash Cars Beat Dealer Loans

A $4,500 cash car avoids 3 years of 12.5% dealer interest and forced full insurance. Always do a $2 PPSR check first.

EV Only Saves If You Charge At Home

Home off-peak EV charging is ~2.5× cheaper than petrol; public fast chargers cost about the same as petrol.

What Will I Learn in Cars & Driving?

(15 topics)

Queensland uses a Graduated Licensing Scheme (GLS) designed to build safe driving habits over time: 1) Age 15 & 11 months: Enrol in the online PrepL road rules course ($29.70). 2) Age 16: Pass PrepL and obtain your Learner (L) licence ($80.15 for 3 years). Hold for at least 12 months and complete 100 logbook hours (including at least 10 at night). 3) Age 17: Pass the online Hazard Perception Test ($42.70) and the practical driving test ($69.40) to earn your Provisional P1 (Red P) licence ($94.65/1yr). 4) Age 18: Move to Provisional P2 (Green P) licence ($132.00/2yrs) after holding P1 for 12 months with a clean record. 5) Age 20: Upgrade to a full Open Licence ($94.65/1yr or $212.00/5yrs). Total mandatory government test and licence fees from Ls to your first Open licence equal ~$543.25.
